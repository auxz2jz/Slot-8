## Change

Dense fusion now filters candidate pairs for actual shared-world transform availability before choosing the distributed up-to-six attempts. Eligibility requires both image files, both refined camera centers, and a recovered world pose for the first camera. The previous generic missing-pose-or-image diagnostic has been replaced with precise prerequisite-specific messages.
