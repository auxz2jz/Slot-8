## Purpose

Use ordinary US Letter 8.5x11 paper for accurate multi-sheet calibration boards without leaving one sheet covering another in the finished calibration field.

## New large photo target

- New preset: **Large 4-Letter (38.1 mm)**.
- 9x6 inner corners / 10x7 squares.
- Exact square size: **38.1 mm = 1.5 in**.
- Checkerboard: **15.0 x 10.5 in**.
- Finished white-border board: **16.0 x 11.5 in**.
- Four US Letter landscape pages: A1/A2/B1/B2.
- Existing 25 mm Letter/Legal/Ledger/12x18 targets remain unchanged.
- OpenCV camera-calibration object points use the selected preset's `squareSizeMm`, so the new large target is solved with 38.1 mm geometry.

## Pin-align seam method

- Adjacent pages share a 0.5 in total printed overlap around internal seams.
- Matching registration crosshairs have a center dot intended for a small pin/pinhole.
- Registration marks sit on sacrificial tabs outside the final kept tile rectangle.
- After pin alignment, a straightedge can be placed through the matching pin centers and both overlapping sheets cut together.
- Excess strips are removed and the fresh edges are butted together.
- No registration crosshair or paper overlap needs to remain over the final active calibration artwork.

## Laser target

- LEFT and RIGHT remain the original 12x18 in target geometry.
- Marker size remains 35.0 mm.
- The existing tiled preset ID is retained for backward selection compatibility.
- Its PDF assets are upgraded to the pin-align seam method.
